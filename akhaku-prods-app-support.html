AKHAKU PRODS Help & Support

Getting started, troubleshooting, and support for AKHAKU PRODS.

iOS & Android · AKHAKU PRODS

Contact AKHAKU PRODS

akhakuprods@gmail.com

Email us for app support, feedback, accessibility help, or privacy questions.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, what happened, and the steps that caused it. A redacted screenshot can help. Never send passwords, payment information, verification codes, private seat tokens, or recovery phrases.

01 · App help

Explore the portfolio

Browse the public apps and websites, open a project for details and screenshots, and use its available links to learn more. You do not need an administrator account to browse public work.

02 · App help

The portfolio has not refreshed

Check your connection, reopen the app, or use its refresh controls. A previously cached portfolio can remain visible offline, so recent changes may need a successful refresh.

03 · App help

Contact the studio

Use the app contact options for a project enquiry or email the address below for technical support. Check your connection if the contact form cannot send. Do not submit confidential credentials or private business records.

04 · App help

Display and accessibility

Open Settings to adjust the available appearance, text, contrast, and motion preferences. Include a screenshot and the relevant setting when reporting a readability issue.

05 · App help

Private administration

Administrator access is separate from public browsing. Keep private project and vault details out of support messages. Do not reset app storage as a first troubleshooting step for a private-data problem.

Privacy questions

Read the AKHAKU PRODS privacy policy for information about storage, connected services, and your choices.
