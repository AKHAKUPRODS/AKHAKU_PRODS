Ziarat Companion Help & Support

Getting started, troubleshooting, and support for Ziarat Companion.

iOS & Android · AKHAKU PRODS

Contact AKHAKU PRODS

akhakuprods@gmail.com

Email us for app support, feedback, accessibility help, or privacy questions.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, what happened, and the steps that caused it. A redacted screenshot can help. Never send passwords, payment information, verification codes, private seat tokens, or recovery phrases.

01 · App help

Prepare your journey

Browse Places to explore available ziarat destinations and save places you want to visit. Use Trip to organise your journey and checklists to prepare for travel and visits.

02 · App help

Read duas and recitations

Use Recite to open a text and the available Arabic, transliteration, and translation. Reading and appearance settings can make longer texts easier to follow.

03 · App help

Personal reflections

Use the available personal-dua, names-to-remember, and journal features to keep your own notes. Saved personal information stays in local app storage in the current implementation.

04 · App help

Content corrections

For a suspected error, include the place or recitation name and the relevant passage or source link. Religious texts, recommended acts, and historical notes should be checked with trusted scholars and authentic sources.

05 · App help

Offline content and external links

Built-in content can be read without a continuous connection, while external source and map links may require internet access. Avoid deleting the app when troubleshooting local notes, as they may not be recoverable.

Privacy questions

Read the Ziarat Companion privacy policy for information about storage, connected services, and your choices.
