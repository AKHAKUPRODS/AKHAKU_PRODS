Privacy Policy

Mafia Moderator

§ 01

Overview

Mafia Moderator is a game-management utility for running the social deduction game Mafia (also known as Werewolf). This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Short version: Mafia Moderator does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

§ 02

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or share any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioural data
  • Player names or game session data
  • Photos, microphone input, or camera data
  • Contacts or calendar information

§ 03

Local Data Storage

Any data the app stores — such as game settings, role configurations, or player lists — is saved locally on your device only. This data is not transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

§ 04

Third-Party Services

Mafia Moderator does not integrate with or send data to any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

If the app is distributed through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, their own policies govern data collected through those platforms. We have no control over and no access to that data.

§ 05

Permissions

The app may request certain device permissions solely to enable gameplay features (such as sound or screen wake lock). No permission is used to collect or transmit personal data. Any permission usage is limited strictly to the feature it supports.

§ 06

Children's Privacy

Mafia Moderator is suitable for general audiences including children. Because we do not collect any personal data from any user, there is no special risk to younger players. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13, nor from any user of any age.

§ 07

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
