Privacy Policy

Passport Pages

Platform: iOS & Android · Travel Journal & Passport Manager

Effective:  ·  Developer: AKHAKU PRODS

Sign-in · Cloud Sync

§ 01

Overview

Passport Pages is a personal travel journal app that lets you save travel memories, photos, and documents.

Short version: We do not collect, view, or share your memories, photos, or documents. Sign-in is optional and exists solely for your own sync convenience.

§ 02

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identification information beyond what is needed for optional sign-in
  • Your memories, journal entries, photos, or documents — we have no access to these
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics

§ 03

Sign-in & Cloud Sync

Users may optionally sign in to back up and sync their travel memories across devices. Sign-in credentials are used only to identify your personal data store. Your content remains private and is never accessed by AKHAKU PRODS.

§ 04

Your Memories, Your Data

All content you save — including photos, documents, and journal entries — is stored for your personal use only. We have no access to your data and do not process or view it in any way. Deleting the app or your account permanently removes your data.

§ 05

Photo & Document Storage

Photos and documents are stored solely to provide the app's functionality for you. They are never used for AI training, advertising, or any other purpose, and are never shared with or accessible by AKHAKU PRODS.

§ 06

Third-Party Services

Passport Pages does not integrate with advertising networks, analytics platforms, or data brokers. If distributed via Google Play, Google's own policies govern data collected through the Play Store platform.
