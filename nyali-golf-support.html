Nyali Golf Companion Help & Support

Getting started, troubleshooting, and support for Nyali Golf Companion.

iOS & Android · AKHAKU PRODS

Contact AKHAKU PRODS

akhakuprods@gmail.com

Email us for app support, feedback, accessibility help, or privacy questions.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, what happened, and the steps that caused it. A redacted screenshot can help. Never send passwords, payment information, verification codes, private seat tokens, or recovery phrases.

01 · App help

Track a round

Start a round and enter your scores hole by hole using the app controls. Review entries before finishing so that your scorecard reflects what you played.

02 · App help

Explore the course

Open the course view or map to review the holes and course layout. Try zooming a course image if details are difficult to read.

03 · App help

Scorecard corrections

Check that you are viewing the intended round and hole before changing a score. For a calculation problem, include the affected hole, the entered score, and what total you expected.

04 · App help

Saved rounds or sync

If your version offers sign-in and sync, check the connection and that you are using the same account. Avoid uninstalling the app while investigating missing rounds; data saved only on the device may be lost.

05 · App help

Club matters

Use Nyali Golf & Country Club contacts for bookings, membership, handicaps, and official competition results. AKHAKU PRODS can help with technical app problems.

Privacy questions

Read the Nyali Golf Companion privacy policy for information about storage, connected services, and your choices.
