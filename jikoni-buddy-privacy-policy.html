Privacy Policy

Jikoni Buddy

How Jikoni Buddy handles information when you use the app.

AKHAKU PRODS

§ 01

Overview

Jikoni Buddy is a personal recipe manager app. This policy explains how your data is handled.

Short version: We do not collect, view, or share your recipes or personal data. Sign-in is optional and exists solely for your own convenience.

§ 02

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Your recipe content — we cannot read or access recipes you create
  • Device identifiers, usage analytics, or behavioural data
  • Location data
  • Photos or media beyond what you choose to attach to your own recipes

§ 03

Sign-in & Cloud Sync

Users may optionally sign in to sync their recipes across devices. Sign-in credentials are used only to identify your personal data store — we do not use them for any other purpose. Your recipes remain private and are never accessed by AKHAKU PRODS.

§ 04

Your Recipes, Your Data

All recipes you create are stored for your use only. If you sign in, your recipes are backed up securely and accessible exclusively by you. Deleting the app or your account permanently removes your data.

§ 05

Third-Party Services

Jikoni Buddy does not integrate with advertising networks, analytics platforms, or data brokers. If distributed via Google Play, Google's own policies govern data collected through the Play Store platform.

§ 06

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us at: akhakuprods@gmail.com
