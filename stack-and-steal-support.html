Stack and Steal Help & Support

Questions, feedback, or a problem at the table? We can help.

iOS & Android · AKHAKU PRODS

Email us for app support, feedback, accessibility help, or privacy questions.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, what happened, and the steps that caused it. A redacted screenshot can help. Never send passwords, payment information, verification codes, private seat tokens, or recovery phrases.

01 · App help

Play with friends

Enter a display name and choose Create a table or Start a private game, depending on your version. Share the six-character code with your friends; they choose Join with a code. The host starts when everyone is ready. Tables support 2–6 players.

02 · App help

Find other players

Choose Play online or Join an online game, then choose a game mode. Public tables wait for another person and start after a countdown once at least two people are present. Bots are not added to public tables.

03 · App help

Practice offline

Choose Practice with bots, or start a private game with Bots only. Pick the number of bots and their speed. Practice saves automatically and does not need an internet connection. Starting a new practice game can replace an unfinished saved match.

04 · App help

Connection trouble and rejoining

Check Wi-Fi or mobile data, use the latest app version, and reopen the matching online or private-game entry. An active unfinished match can offer Rejoin game after the server verifies your saved seat. Waiting or finished games may not offer rejoin. The current release connects to the normal online service automatically; there is no need to enter a server address.

05 · App help

Scores and game modes

Only stacked cards score. Quick Game plays one round; Race to a Million carries scores across rounds. A late steal can change the lead until the round ends. Open the table menu and Scores & breakdown to see exact totals.

06 · App help

Appearance, sounds, and haptics

Open Settings & Stats, then Settings, to adjust appearance, turn alerts, card sounds, touch haptics, and bot speed. Sounds respect silent mode, and vibration and haptics depend on device support.

07 · App help

Report a game problem safely

Include the game mode, table code if available, what happened, and the steps that caused it. Hide any private hand information you do not want to share. Never send passwords, payment information, or private seat tokens.

Privacy questions

Read the Stack and Steal privacy policy for information about storage, connected services, and your choices.
