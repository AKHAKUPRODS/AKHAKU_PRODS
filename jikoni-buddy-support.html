Jikoni Buddy Help & Support

Getting started, troubleshooting, and support for Jikoni Buddy.

iOS & Android · AKHAKU PRODS

Contact AKHAKU PRODS

akhakuprods@gmail.com

Email us for app support, feedback, accessibility help, or privacy questions.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, what happened, and the steps that caused it. A redacted screenshot can help. Never send passwords, payment information, verification codes, private seat tokens, or recovery phrases.

01 · App help

Save and organise recipes

Open Recipes to add your own recipe, with ingredients, measurements, instructions, notes, and servings. Cuisine and course filters help you find it again. Review imported recipes before saving or cooking them.

02 · App help

Choose what to cook

Use Cook Now to check what you have available. Mark ingredients as Have or Buy before cooking; Buy items can be added to your shopping list. Keep your pantry up to date for more useful suggestions.

03 · App help

Meal planning and shopping

Assign recipes to the weekly meal plan, then generate a grouped shopping list. You can tick off items while shopping and add extra items yourself.

04 · App help

An imported recipe looks wrong

Some websites format recipes differently. Check the source link and edit the imported ingredients and steps before saving. Send us the public recipe URL and a description of the issue; do not send sign-in details.

05 · App help

Tutorial and app help

The tutorial can be opened again from More / Settings. The app also has Help with searchable FAQs and an offline answer guide. Check appearance settings if you prefer a light or dark screen while cooking.

Privacy questions

Read the Jikoni Buddy privacy policy for information about storage, connected services, and your choices.
