Privacy Policy

Stack and Steal

Your choices, saved games, and online table information.

Effective: 22 October 2026 · Web page updated: 24 October 2026

01 · Information and local storage

What the app uses

Stack and Steal does not require an email account or password to play. You choose a display name. Offline practice, preferences, statistics, and saved game information are stored on your device. An online-seat credential is kept in secure device storage (the Keychain on iOS) so you can reconnect.

02 · Multiplayer

Online play

Online games send your chosen display name, a generated player identifier, table code, and game actions to our Cloudflare-hosted service. Table state includes cards, stacks, scores, and recent game events, allowing the server to validate moves and restore a table. Other players can see your display name and public game state; the service sends each player only their own private hand.

Private games are accessed using a table code. Public matchmaking connects you with other online players. Use an alias rather than a real name if you prefer, and share private table codes only with people you want to join.

03 · Retention and service providers

How long online records remain

Online tables are scheduled for deletion after seven days without game activity. Active table records may remain while the game is in use. Infrastructure providers may process network information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the service. The game service also uses IP addresses temporarily in memory for request rate limiting. Provider security and technical logs may have separate retention. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

04 · Tracking and purchases

Advertising and analytics

The current app has no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app advertising tracking. This version has no in-app purchase flow. Local game statistics are used to show your own results and are not a cross-device analytics account.

05 · Support and your choices

You choose how to play

You can play offline to avoid sending game information to the online service. Removing a saved online seat or clearing its local credential does not immediately delete the server’s table record. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove credentials retained by the operating system or existing server records.

If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to include so we can respond. For questions or data requests, contact akhakuprods@gmail.com. If your request concerns an online table, include its code if available. Do not send passwords, payment details, or your private seat token. Support correspondence may be retained to respond and follow up.

06 · This page and future changes

Website and policy updates

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers receive standard web requests when you visit. The website privacy policy covers the portfolio site separately. Features can vary by platform and app version. We will update this page when the app’s data practices change.
