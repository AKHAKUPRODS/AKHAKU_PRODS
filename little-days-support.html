Little Days Help & Support

Getting started, troubleshooting, and support for Little Days.

iOS & Android · AKHAKU PRODS

Contact AKHAKU PRODS

akhakuprods@gmail.com

Email us for app support, feedback, accessibility help, or privacy questions.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, what happened, and the steps that caused it. A redacted screenshot can help. Never send passwords, payment information, verification codes, private seat tokens, or recovery phrases.

01 · App help

Start your family journal

Set up your baby journey and add notes, appointments, scans, photos, milestones, and growth records from the calendar. Use Timeline and Gallery to revisit saved moments.

02 · App help

Choose local use or family sharing

Local mode keeps memories on the device. If you choose a synced family space, sign in and invite only people you trust. Check that both people have joined the same space when investigating missing shared entries.

03 · App help

Extra Private mode

Keep your family recovery phrase safe. Extra Private mode encrypts supported content before sync; losing the phrase can prevent recovery. Never email the recovery phrase or encryption keys to support.

04 · App help

Missing photos or memories

Check the selected date, active family space, connection, and sign-in account. Avoid clearing local data or uninstalling during troubleshooting, particularly if memories have not synced or you use local mode.

05 · App help

Exports and privacy requests

Use the available memory-book export controls to save a copy of your entries. Contact support for account or cloud-data deletion help; uninstalling the app alone does not delete a synced space. Redact family names, dates, and health details from screenshots.

Privacy questions

Read the Little Days privacy policy for information about storage, connected services, and your choices.
