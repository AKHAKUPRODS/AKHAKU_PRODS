Privacy Policy

Ziarat Companion

How Ziarat Companion handles information when you use the app.

Effective: 16 October 2026 · AKHAKU PRODS

Your saved places, trips, duas, checklists, and reflections are stored locally. If you send a message through Contact Us, the details you submit are processed by Formspree and delivered to AKHAKU PRODS.

Section 01

Information you enter

The app stores saved and visited places, recitation bookmarks and recent reading activity, guided-ziarat progress, checklists, trip details, custom places, personal duas, names to remember, and journal entries. These records can reveal personal travel plans or religious interests, so include only information you are comfortable keeping on your device.

Section 02

Local storage and use

Your saved information is used to personalise your journey, organise trips, and remember progress. The current app stores it locally and does not operate an account or cloud-sync service for these records. Your device’s backup settings may create separate copies.

Section 03

Contact messages

If you use Contact Us, the app sends the name and email address you choose to provide, request type, related place or recitation, and message to Formspree. We use this information to respond to suggestions, corrections, and support requests. Formspree may also process technical request information needed to deliver and protect the service. See Formspree’s privacy policy.

Section 04

Maps and external sources

Source and map links may open third-party services, including religious reference sites and mapping providers. Those services handle network requests and any information you choose to provide under their own policies. A link to a place is not permission for the app to continuously track your location.

Section 05

Advertising and tracking

The current app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Personal journal entries and saved religious activity are not automatically sent with a contact message; avoid including sensitive content in a message unless it is needed for your request.

Section 06

Your choices and retention

Use the available app controls to manage saved items. Local information remains until removed or the app’s stored data is deleted; device backups may retain copies. Support messages may be retained to respond and follow up. You can contact us about correspondence you have sent. We cannot delete or recover records that exist only on your device.

Section 07

Children’s privacy

This app is intended for general audiences and is not designed to collect information directly from children. Parents or guardians can contact us about information they believe was provided to us by a child.

Section 08

About this policy page

This policy covers the app’s current features; availability can vary by platform and version. This web page is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads Google Fonts, whose providers may process standard network request information. The website privacy policy covers this portfolio website separately.

Section 09

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or data handling change. The effective date above identifies this version. For privacy questions, corrections, or data requests, contact AKHAKU PRODS at akhakuprods@gmail.com. Please name the app in your message and avoid sending private records unless needed for your request.
