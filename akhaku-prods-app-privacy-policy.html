Privacy Policy

AKHAKU PRODS

How AKHAKU PRODS handles information when you use the app.

Effective: 16 October 2026 · AKHAKU PRODS

You can browse the public portfolio without a visitor account. Public project content is delivered through Apple CloudKit. Messages you submit are processed by Formspree; private administration data is kept separate from the public portfolio.

Section 01

Public portfolio browsing

The app displays project descriptions, icons, screenshots, links, and other information the studio has marked for public display. It fetches a shared public portfolio snapshot through Apple CloudKit and may retain a local cache for offline browsing. Viewing public projects does not require creating an AKHAKU PRODS visitor account.

Section 02

Contact form and support

If you use the contact form, the information you enter is sent to Formspree and delivered to AKHAKU PRODS. This can include your name, email address, project or enquiry details, and message. We use it to respond to your enquiry and provide support. Formspree may process technical request information as part of providing the service. See Formspree’s privacy policy.

Section 03

Private studio administration

Authorised studio users can store private project, business, revenue, cost, note, and vault information. This information is protected separately from the public browsing experience. The app writes encrypted private records to local storage and, where enabled, the studio user’s iCloud storage. Only content selected for public publication is included in the public portfolio snapshot.

Section 04

Apple services and device security

CloudKit, iCloud storage, and device authentication are provided by Apple. Their operation is subject to your Apple account and device settings and Apple’s privacy policy. Device biometrics are handled by the operating system; the app does not receive your facial or fingerprint template.

Section 05

External links, advertising, and tracking

Project links can open app stores, websites, or email applications with their own privacy practices. The current app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Public project information is intended to be visible to portfolio visitors; private notes and vault records are not included in the public snapshot.

Section 06

Your choices and retention

You can clear local app data or remove the app. Authorised administrators manage private records and what is published publicly. Removing the app does not itself delete iCloud records, public portfolio content, or messages already sent. Contact us about support correspondence or public information requiring correction or removal. Device backups and third-party services may retain separate copies according to their settings and policies.

Section 07

Children’s privacy

This app is intended for general audiences and is not designed to collect information directly from children. Parents or guardians can contact us about information they believe was provided to us by a child.

Section 08

About this policy page

This policy covers the app’s current features; availability can vary by platform and version. This web page is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads Google Fonts, whose providers may process standard network request information. The website privacy policy covers this portfolio website separately.

Section 09

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or data handling change. The effective date above identifies this version. For privacy questions, corrections, or data requests, contact AKHAKU PRODS at akhakuprods@gmail.com. Please name the app in your message and avoid sending private records unless needed for your request.
