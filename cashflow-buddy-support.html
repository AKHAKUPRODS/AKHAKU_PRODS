Cashflow Buddy Help & Support

Getting started, troubleshooting, and support for Cashflow Buddy.

iOS & Android · AKHAKU PRODS

Contact AKHAKU PRODS

akhakuprods@gmail.com

Email us for app support, feedback, accessibility help, or privacy questions.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, what happened, and the steps that caused it. A redacted screenshot can help. Never send passwords, payment information, verification codes, private seat tokens, or recovery phrases.

01 · App help

Record income and expenses

Use Add entry to choose income or expense, then enter the amount, date, category, payment method, and any note you need. Check the selected currency and entry type before saving.

02 · App help

Understand your budget

Set your monthly budget and review Home and Summary. Date filters change which entries are included, so check the selected month or period if a total looks unexpected.

03 · App help

Categories and recurring templates

Use More to manage expense categories, income sources, and recurring templates. A saved template helps you record repeat transactions; review the date and amount when using it.

04 · App help

Export your records

Use Export to create a CSV of your entries and save it somewhere you control. Exported files may contain private financial notes, so check the destination before sharing.

05 · App help

Protect local data

The current app stores budget records locally. Household cloud sharing is not active in this implementation. Export important records before changing devices or removing the app; support cannot recover entries that existed only on your device.

Privacy questions

Read the Cashflow Buddy privacy policy for information about storage, connected services, and your choices.
