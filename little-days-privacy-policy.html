App Privacy Policy

Little Days

How Little Days handles the information you choose to use with the app.

Effective: 16 October 2026  /  Developer: AKHAKU PRODS

You can keep your family memories on your device or choose a synced family space. Shared spaces use Firebase and are accessible to their members. Optional Extra Private mode encrypts supported family content before it is uploaded.

Section 01

Family information and memories

Depending on what you enter, the app stores baby or family names, pregnancy stage, expected or actual birth dates, appointments, scans, milestones, growth records, notes, and photos. This may include sensitive family and health-related information. You control which details and photos you add.

Section 02

Local use and family sync

Local mode keeps your memories in app storage on your device. If you choose family sync, the app uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to authenticate members and store your family space, profiles, entries, and shared content. A synced space is private to its members rather than a public social feed. Service providers process cloud data to deliver these features.

Section 03

Sign-in and family membership

Supported sign-in methods include Apple, Google, email, phone, and anonymous authentication. Depending on your choice, the authentication service processes an account identifier and information such as your email address, phone number, or display name. Family-space identifiers, membership roles, and invite codes connect members to the appropriate space. Invite only people you trust: members can access content available to their family space.

Section 04

Extra Private encryption

Extra Private mode encrypts supported baby-profile, memory, and growth content on your device before cloud sync, using your family recovery phrase. Account and family membership information still needs to be processed to provide the service. Keep the recovery phrase secure; without it, encrypted content may not be recoverable. Ordinary Private Sync and Extra Private mode have different recovery and privacy properties.

Section 05

Photos, exports, and device lock

The photo picker lets you choose images to add to your memories; selected photos become part of the saved content and may sync when sharing is enabled. Exports create a separate copy that you can share using your device. App-lock authentication is handled by the operating system, and the app does not receive your biometric template. Shared or exported copies cannot be recalled by deleting an entry in the app.

Section 06

Service providers and technical data

Google Firebase processes authentication and cloud-service data, including technical information used for security and service operation. See Firebase’s privacy information and Google’s privacy policy. Apple sign-in and device services are covered by Apple’s privacy policy. The current app does not include advertising or a Google Analytics integration.

Section 07

Your choices, deletion, and retention

You can manage and delete entries with the available app controls. Clearing local data or uninstalling the app does not itself delete a synced family space or authentication account. Contact us for help with account or cloud-data deletion requests. Cloud records remain until removed through the relevant service or request; provider backups and exported copies may have separate retention. Support messages you send us may be retained to respond and follow up.

Section 08

Children and family privacy

Little Days is designed for parents and caregivers to record a family journey. It is not a social network for children. Add information about a child only if you are entitled to do so, and consider the privacy of your child and other family members before inviting people or exporting memories.

Section 09

About this policy page

This policy covers the app’s current features; availability can vary by platform and version. This web page is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads Google Fonts, whose providers may process standard network request information. The website privacy policy covers this portfolio website separately.

Section 10

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or data handling change. The effective date above identifies this version. For privacy questions, corrections, or data requests, contact AKHAKU PRODS at akhakuprods@outlook.com. Please name the app in your message and avoid sending private records unless needed for your request.
