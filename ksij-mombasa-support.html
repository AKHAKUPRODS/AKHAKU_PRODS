KSIJ Mombasa Help & Support

Getting started, troubleshooting, and support for KSIJ Mombasa.

iOS & Android · AKHAKU PRODS

Contact AKHAKU PRODS

akhakuprods@gmail.com

Email us for app support, feedback, accessibility help, or privacy questions.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, what happened, and the steps that caused it. A redacted screenshot can help. Never send passwords, payment information, verification codes, private seat tokens, or recovery phrases.

01 · App help

Community information

Use the app to view KSIJ Mombasa news, announcements, events, prayer information, and community resources. Some content needs an internet connection to refresh.

02 · App help

Content is missing or out of date

Check your connection, reopen the app, and refresh using the available controls. If the problem continues, tell us which section is affected and the date of the information you expected to see.

03 · App help

Prayer times and calendar questions

Check your device date and time settings. For questions about a timetable, community announcement, or religious date, confirm the information with the Jamaat. Include the displayed date and section when reporting a technical mismatch.

04 · App help

Donation and external links

Links may open another website or app. Check that your browser and connection are working. Never email payment-card details, passwords, or donation receipts containing private information unless the authorised recipient specifically requires them.

05 · App help

Technical help or community enquiries

Use the email below for app faults and accessibility help. For membership, events, or community decisions, use the Jamaat contact details shown inside the app.

Privacy questions

Read the KSIJ Mombasa privacy policy for information about storage, connected services, and your choices.
