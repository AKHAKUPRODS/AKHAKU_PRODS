Privacy Policy

Nyali Golf Companion

How Nyali Golf Companion handles information when you use the app.

AKHAKU PRODS

§ 01

Overview

The Nyali Golf & Country Club Companion app helps members track golf scores and rounds at Nyali Golf & Country Club.

Short version: We do not collect, view, or share your scorecards or personal data. Sign-in is optional and exists solely for your own sync convenience.

§ 02

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identification information beyond what is needed for optional sign-in
  • Your scorecard or round data — we cannot read or access scores you save
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics
  • Any data shared with Nyali Golf & Country Club or third parties

§ 03

Sign-in & Cloud Sync

Users may optionally sign in to sync their scorecards across devices. Sign-in credentials are used only to identify your personal data store. Your scores remain private and are never accessed by AKHAKU PRODS or Nyali Golf & Country Club.

§ 04

Your Scores, Your Data

All scorecards and round history you save are for your use only. If you sign in, your data is backed up securely and is accessible exclusively by you. Deleting the app or your account permanently removes your data.

§ 05

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with advertising networks, analytics platforms, or data brokers. If distributed via Google Play, Google's own policies govern data collected through the Play Store platform.

§ 06

Contact
