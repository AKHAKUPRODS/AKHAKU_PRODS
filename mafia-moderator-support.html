Mafia Moderator Help & Support

Getting started, troubleshooting, and support for Mafia Moderator.

iOS & Android · AKHAKU PRODS

Contact AKHAKU PRODS

akhakuprods@gmail.com

Email us for app support, feedback, accessibility help, or privacy questions.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, what happened, and the steps that caused it. A redacted screenshot can help. Never send passwords, payment information, verification codes, private seat tokens, or recovery phrases.

01 · App help

Run a game night

Add your players, choose the roles for your group, and let the app help you run the night and day phases. Use the moderator controls to follow the round and record what happened.

02 · App help

Roles and player setup

Check player names and role choices before starting. Keep role information hidden from other players when you pass the device. If the setup is wrong, return to setup and correct it before dealing roles.

03 · App help

Round controls and sound

Use the on-screen prompts and timers to move through the game. Check the device volume, silent mode, and app settings if a sound does not play. Keep the app open while moderating.

04 · App help

A game is not behaving as expected

Tell us which phase you were in, the selected roles, player count, and the last action before the problem. A screenshot can help, but hide player identities or private role information you do not want to share.

05 · App help

Local settings and game data

Avoid uninstalling or clearing app storage while a game is in progress. Local settings and saved information may be removed. There is no guarantee of recovering data that was only saved on that device.

Privacy questions

Read the Mafia Moderator privacy policy for information about storage, connected services, and your choices.
