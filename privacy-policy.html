Privacy Policy

AKHAKU PRODS Website

This policy explains how privacy is handled on the AKHAKU PRODS portfolio website. Individual apps may have separate privacy policies linked from their app cards and footer links.

Effective: 30 May 2026  /  Developer: AKHAKU PRODS

Short version: this website is a static portfolio. AKHAKU PRODS does not run accounts, contact forms, advertising cookies, or analytics on this site.

Section 01

Information We Collect

AKHAKU PRODS does not directly collect personal information through this website. The site does not include user accounts, payments, newsletter signup forms, comment areas, or in-page contact forms.

If you contact AKHAKU PRODS by email, the information you choose to include in that email will be received and used only to respond to your message.

Section 02

Cookies and Analytics

AKHAKU PRODS does not set advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics scripts on this website.

Section 03

Third-Party Services

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may process standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and request logs, according to their own privacy practices.

  • GitHub Pages hosts and serves the website files.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces used in the website design.
  • External links, including app stores, other websites, and email links, are governed by the privacy practices of those services.

Section 04

App Privacy Policies

The portfolio links to privacy policies for individual AKHAKU PRODS apps and client apps. Those policies describe the data handling for each app and are separate from this website policy.

Section 05

Data Retention

Email messages sent to AKHAKU PRODS may be retained as needed to respond to support requests, maintain business records, or follow up on product-related questions. You may request deletion of your email correspondence where practical and legally permitted.

Section 06

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Section 07

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Section 08

Contact

For questions about this website privacy policy, contact AKHAKU PRODS at akhakuprods@outlook.com.
