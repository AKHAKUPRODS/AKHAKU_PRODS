Privacy Policy

AKHAKU PRODS Website

Privacy information for this portfolio website and its contact form. Individual apps have their own policies.

Effective: 8 June 2026 · AKHAKU PRODS

Short version: this website does not run user accounts, advertising cookies, or analytics. If you use the contact form, the details you submit are processed by Formspree and delivered to AKHAKU PRODS.

Section 01

Information We Collect

If you use the website contact form, we receive the name, email address, subject, and message you choose to submit. This information is used to respond to your enquiry, provide support, or discuss a potential project.

If you contact AKHAKU PRODS directly by email, the information you include in that email is used for the same purposes. The website does not include user accounts, payments, newsletters, or comment areas.

Section 02

Cookies and Analytics

AKHAKU PRODS does not set advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics scripts on this website.

Section 03

Third-Party Services

The website uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy practices.

  • GitHub Pages hosts and serves the website files. GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces used in the website design.
  • Formspree processes contact form submissions and forwards them to AKHAKU PRODS. Submitted form details and standard technical request information may be processed by Formspree.
  • External links, including app stores, other websites, and email links, are governed by the privacy practices of those services.

Section 04

App Privacy Policies

The portfolio links to privacy policies for individual AKHAKU PRODS apps and client apps. Those policies describe the data handling for each app and are separate from this website policy.

Section 05

Data Retention

Contact form submissions and email messages may be retained by AKHAKU PRODS and Formspree as needed to respond to enquiries, provide support, maintain business records, prevent abuse, or follow up on product-related questions. You may request deletion of your correspondence where practical and legally permitted.

Section 06

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Section 07

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Section 08

Contact

For questions about this website privacy policy, contact AKHAKU PRODS at akhakuprods@gmail.com.
