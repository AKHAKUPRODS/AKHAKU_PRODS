Passport Pages Help & Support

Getting started, troubleshooting, and support for Passport Pages.

iOS & Android · AKHAKU PRODS

Contact AKHAKU PRODS

akhakuprods@gmail.com

Email us for app support, feedback, accessibility help, or privacy questions.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, what happened, and the steps that caused it. A redacted screenshot can help. Never send passwords, payment information, verification codes, private seat tokens, or recovery phrases.

01 · App help

Keep a travel journal

Create a trip, add its dates and places, and record memories as you travel. Use the app controls to organise your entries and return to an existing trip.

02 · App help

Photos and documents

Use the available photo or document picker to attach a file. If an attachment will not open, check that the original file is available on your device and that access has not been revoked. Try a smaller file if the device is low on storage.

03 · App help

Map and place information

Some map or linked services need a connection. Check the saved destination details and device connection if a place is not displayed as expected.

04 · App help

Missing memories or sync

If your app version offers cloud sync, confirm that you are signed in to the intended account and allow the connection to finish. Do not delete the app as a first troubleshooting step: memories stored only on that device may be removed.

05 · App help

Safe support requests

Describe the affected screen and file type without sending passport numbers, identity documents, boarding passes, private photos, or account credentials. Use a redacted screenshot where possible.

Privacy questions

Read the Passport Pages privacy policy for information about storage, connected services, and your choices.
