Privacy Policy

KSIJ Mombasa

How KSIJ Mombasa handles information when you use the app.

AKHAKU PRODS

§ 01

Overview

KSIJ Mombasa is a community information app for The Khoja Shia Ithna Asheri Jamaat Mombasa. This policy explains how the app handles data.

Short version: KSIJ Mombasa collects absolutely no user data. There is no sign-in, no account, no tracking, and no analytics of any kind.

§ 02

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identification information of any kind
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioural data
  • Any user-generated content — there is none

§ 03

No Sign-in Required

KSIJ Mombasa functions entirely without an account or sign-in. It is a read-only information resource providing community news, announcements, and resources to members.

§ 04

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with advertising networks, analytics platforms, or any data-collecting third-party SDKs. If distributed via Google Play, Google's own policies govern data collected through the Play Store platform.

§ 05

Children's Privacy

Because no data is collected from any user, there is no risk to younger users. We do not knowingly collect information from children or adults alike.

§ 06

Contact
