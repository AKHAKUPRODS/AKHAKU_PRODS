App Privacy Policy

Cashflow Buddy

How Cashflow Buddy handles the information you choose to use with the app.

Effective: 16 October 2026  /  Developer: AKHAKU PRODS

Your budget entries are stored on your device. Cashflow Buddy does not require an account or connect to your bank. You choose whether to export or share your records.

Section 01

Information you enter

The app stores the income and expenses you record, including amounts, dates, titles, categories, payment-method labels, and notes. It also stores your budget, recurring templates, currency, and display preferences. Naming a payment method does not give the app access to a bank, card, or mobile-money account.

Section 02

How your information is used

Your entries are used to show your transaction history, calculate spending and income summaries, and compare spending with your budget. The current app stores these records locally and does not send your financial entries to AKHAKU PRODS.

Section 03

Storage and backups

Budget records and settings are saved in local app storage. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your settings. Local storage is not a substitute for keeping your own backup of important records.

Section 04

Exports and sharing

If you choose CSV export, the app creates a file containing your transaction records and opens your device’s sharing options. Only share this file with people or services you trust. Copies saved outside the app remain under your control and are not removed when you delete an app entry.

Section 05

Accounts, advertising, and tracking

The current app does not require user registration, include advertising or analytics SDKs, or automatically import banking transactions. No household cloud-sharing service is active in the current implementation.

Section 06

Your choices and retention

You can manage records using the app’s available controls. Local data remains until it is removed or the app’s stored data is deleted. Device backups and exported files may retain separate copies. We cannot recover financial entries that were only stored on your device. Support correspondence you send us is used to respond to your request and may be retained for follow-up.

Section 07

Children’s privacy

This app is intended for general audiences and is not designed to collect information directly from children. Parents or guardians can contact us about information they believe was provided to us by a child.

Section 08

About this policy page

This policy covers the app’s current features; availability can vary by platform and version. This web page is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads Google Fonts, whose providers may process standard network request information. The website privacy policy covers this portfolio website separately.

Section 09

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or data handling change. The effective date above identifies this version. For privacy questions, corrections, or data requests, contact AKHAKU PRODS at akhakuprods@outlook.com. Please name the app in your message and avoid sending private records unless needed for your request.
