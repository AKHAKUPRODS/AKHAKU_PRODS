Habari Insurance Help & Support

Getting started, troubleshooting, and support for Habari Insurance.

iOS & Android · AKHAKU PRODS

Contact AKHAKU PRODS

akhakuprods@gmail.com

Email us for app support, feedback, accessibility help, or privacy questions.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, what happened, and the steps that caused it. A redacted screenshot can help. Never send passwords, payment information, verification codes, private seat tokens, or recovery phrases.

01 · App help

Find insurance services

Browse the app for Habari Insurance Brokers information, services, and contact options. External service or client-portal pages may open in a browser and need an internet connection.

02 · App help

A page or portal does not load

Check Wi-Fi or mobile data, reopen the page, and try again. Include the page name and error message when reporting a technical fault. Do not share portal passwords or verification codes.

03 · App help

Policies, claims, and quotations

For insurance quotations, cover, claims, renewals, and policy changes, contact Habari Insurance Brokers directly at Insure@habari.co.ke or use the official contact details in the app. This app support page does not handle insurance decisions or claim documents.

04 · App help

Technical and accessibility help

AKHAKU PRODS can help with app installation, display, navigation, and accessibility issues. Tell us which screen is affected and the device and app version.

Privacy questions

Read the Habari Insurance privacy policy for information about storage, connected services, and your choices.
